## Summary

Two or three sentences: what this proposes, for whom.

## Problem

What is wrong or missing today, with examples. Link to `current/` where it
describes today's system.

## Proposal

What the system should do. Start with what someone using or building on it
sees, then how it works: components, their responsibilities, interfaces,
data. Diagrams in Mermaid.

## Rules it follows

How it fits the design's principles (layers, ownership, optional services,
permissions). Say where it bends one, and why.

## Alternatives

What else was considered, and why not.

## Costs and risks

measured.
